Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

What we collect

Account: email and password (handled by Google Firebase). Profile: name, age, gender, college, college ID, city, photo and interests. Activity: squads, join and connect requests, chat messages, marketplace listings (including the WhatsApp number you add), blocks, reports and feedback.

How we use it

To run the app, show squads, people and listings to students of your college, keep users safe and fix problems. We do not sell your data and show no ads.

Who can see what

Your name, city, photo and interests are shown to students of your college. A WhatsApp number added to a listing is visible to people who can see that listing. Other profile details are stored with your profile. Chats are visible to their members.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase and the app is hosted on Netlify. These providers process data to run the service.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, block or report users, and delete your account in the Me tab. Deleting removes your profile, squads and listings. Messages may remain visible to people in chats you were part of.

Age

UniSide is not for people under 18.

Contact

Use Help and feedback in the app (Me tab).
